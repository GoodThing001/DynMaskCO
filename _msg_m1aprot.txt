TWO-STAGE DESIGN, WITH THE FIRST-STAGE PASS CONDITION MADE EXPLICIT
Stage 1 calls compose_certification(..., quality_check_fn=None, project_quality=False)
and is judged by an INDEPENDENT predicate, never by rep['installable']. That matters
because stage 1 always passes None, so the composer always reports
quality.state == CAPABILITY_GAP_BLOCKING and installable is False even when every
executable check passes. The predicate checks the four legality gates, repair_plan,
full_partition, route_feasible, energy_budget and the report structure, and it also
asserts the quality state is STILL the capability gap (no silent quality verdict).
Stage 2 is reached only after stage 1 passes: registry admission, applicable-domain
membership at run time, then strict type checking. Final binds both stages AND the
candidate plan identity.
QUALITY PROJECTION RULING: stage 1 passes project_quality=False. With True the composer
appends quality_projection_failed to reasons, which would make a projection whose
conservativeness is NOT established behave as a hard gate - silently promoting an
unproven projection into the certification.

THE THREE CORRECTIONS FROM THE ERRATA ARE IMPLEMENTED, NOT JUST DOCUMENTED
