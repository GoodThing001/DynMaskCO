Ledger + status: the V1.20 execution-entry hardening is recorded; real F batches remain 0

Records the fourth blocker closure in the cross-session ledger and both authoritative status
entries, including the one thing that must not be misread: filling a non-empty `approved_by` in the
authorization JSON is NOT human authorisation. Human approval has to come from an explicit user
decision, so the real F batch count stays at zero until that decision exists.

The entries also carry the platform limitation honestly: `os.chmod` cannot express owner-only on
Windows (the stat mode is synthesised and reports 0o666 for ordinary files), so the 0600 check on
the execution-context secret is enforced only on POSIX; on Windows the secret's privacy rests on the
run directory's ACL and on the secret never entering a command line or any durable record.

Also removes the transient patch/debug scratch scripts from the tree (none are deliverables).
