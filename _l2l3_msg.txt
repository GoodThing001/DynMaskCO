L2/L3 escalation pre-registration draft (NOT executed, NOT authorised)

Delivers the third draft of the pre-registration set the D3 matrix requires. It fixes the two
things that are easiest to get wrong when the layers are written down together:

  * the three-layer escalation is binding: a first-step divergence (L1) is never evidence of a
    certified decision-quality improvement (L2), and a quality gap at fixed states is never
    evidence of an online-utility gain (L3). The spec states each layer's precondition separately
    and forbids substituting one for another.
  * the quality vocabulary is kept in three columns -- candidate / accepted / closed-loop -- with
    the explicit rule that `candidate` may never be called "deployment".

It also carries forward the two hard gates that are not documentation problems: the full C0
certification needs a USER-RULED budget B (no synthetic sentinel, no historical CAL fixture), and
L3 needs fresh development days, an equal-budget protocol and a frozen statistical plan, none of
which may be chosen after seeing results.

No batch, no training, no comparison, no final-seed contact. G0/D8/E2/I3/D2 and stable_online_gain
all unchanged.
