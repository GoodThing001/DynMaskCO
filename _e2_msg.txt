E2 training pre-registration draft (NOT executed, NOT authorised)

Delivers the E2 spec that the minimal-executable list ranked as item 5: "E2 training pre-declaration
(do NOT execute)". Also closes the D3 next-stage item "E2 同任务随机初始化对照 -- 不存在 / 需另行
获准的训练预声明" as a SIGNED-READY DRAFT.

The spec's substance is the single-factor design, and its most important new content is E2-7, now
verified against the source rather than assumed: `mpre_trained.py` builds both `c_adapter` and
`delta_head` as `MLP(..., zero_last=True)`, i.e. their last kernel/bias are
`nnx.initializers.zeros_init()` while the hidden layers draw from the rngs stream. So an E2-rand run
that merely randomises the backbone while leaving those two layers alone would still be confounded
if their hidden-layer initialisation differed; the spec therefore FORCES identical initialisers and
an identical seed for both trainable layers on both arms, and requires machine-checkable evidence
that the two arms share parameter names, shapes and dtypes exactly.

That verification is what makes this draft safe to sign: without it, "pretraining vs random" would
silently also be "zero-last vs random init" and "seed A vs seed B".

Also records: `--seed` is NOT E2 (backbone still loads pretrained); the backbone's training-time
identity remains unverified, so E2 can only give a conditional result under the CURRENT file
identity; and executing E2 still requires its own authorisation plus a frozen budget.

No training, no forward, no comparison. G0/D8/L2/L3/I3/D2 and stable_online_gain all unchanged.
