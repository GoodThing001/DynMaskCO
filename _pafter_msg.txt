P_after_install four-field collection pre-registration draft (NOT executed, NOT authorised)

Delivers the spec the minimal-executable list ranked as item 6. It records precisely which half of
the four-field requirement is now real and which half is still open, because conflating them is the
main way this gate could be over-claimed:

  CLOSED this round, with executable evidence (7/7): a durable install receipt written to a fresh
  destination name, a separate process re-reading it, the recorded plan re-hashed from the
  receipt's own bytes, refusal to write a receipt when the ledger installed nothing or when the
  installed plan is not the certified one, detection of byte tampering and of plan-value tampering.

  STILL OPEN, and stated as such: the receipt records the REQUESTED install and the evidence bound
  to it -- it cannot observe the environment's own `_plan_and_commit`; A-3b (the adapter has no
  committed-prefix guard) is confirmed open by execution; and state-level rollback is not proven
  by a receipt.

The spec therefore treats P_after_install as admissible ONLY from a receipt that passed an
independent re-read, forbids filling it from P_certified or P_proposed at all, and makes "no
receipt" and "a receipt that says something else" distinguishable rather than both reading as None.

No collection, no live install, no forward, no comparison. G0/D8/E2/L2/L3/I3/D2 and
stable_online_gain all unchanged.
